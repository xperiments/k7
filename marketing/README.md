## The five sentences that carry the plan

1. **The device makes no sound.** No DAC, no amplifier, no speaker — audio leaves as a 256 kbps MP3
   stream and something else plays it (`docs/specs/sd-stream-server.md:94`).
2. **There is no object in the repository.** No STL, BOM, schematic or photograph; the shell is 3D
   printed and off-the-shelf, and that STL is the single most valuable missing asset.
3. **The positioning is already right, with one clause missing:** *"it plays to the speakers already
   in the room, and it cannot recommend anything."*
4. **Sell the printed shell first** — plastic and a guide, no electronics in the parcel — publish the
   BOM, keep the firmware free, and let the kit wait for its certification.
5. **The honesty is the differentiator.** The refusals list is the best asset in the repository, and
   the campaign should lead with what the product does not do.

## Decisions confirmed by the owner (2026-11-16)

- The commercial route is a **kit**; the shell is **3D printed**.
- Everything is **off-the-shelf** — no custom PCB, no case tooling.
- The **printed shell is sold on its own**; the buyer sources the electronics.
- The **seller is the owner, personally**.
- **Four printers** — capacity is a formula, not yet a number.
