Trust this player once

xtape serves its app over HTTPS with a certificate of its own. Installing it takes a minute and no account: after this the browser treats the player as a secure site, and the app can open your card and keep its measurements offline.

Download the certificate I have already trusted it — open the app

macOS

  1. Open the downloaded ca.pem; Keychain Access asks which keychain — choose System or login.
  2. Find xtape local CA, double-click it and open Trust.
  3. Set When using this certificate to Always Trust and close the window.
  4. Quit and reopen the browser, then open https://xtape.local.

Windows

  1. Double-click the downloaded ca.pem and choose Install Certificate.
  2. Local Machine, then Place all certificates in the following store.
  3. Choose Trusted Root Certification Authorities and finish.
  4. Restart the browser, then open https://xtape.local.

Linux

In Chrome or Chromium: certutil -d sql:$HOME/.pki/nssdb -A -t "C,," -n "xtape local CA" -i ca.pem then restart the browser. On Firefox, import ca.pem under Settings → Privacy & Security → Certificates → Authorities.

Phones and tablets

iOS and Android each have their own way to install a certificate profile; the player works, but the analysis app is meant for a computer. The stream needs none of this.

The stream needs no certificate

A radio or a car head unit can listen at http://xtape.local/stream — plain HTTP, no trust step, no app.
